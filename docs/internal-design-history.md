# Internal design history

The `research/`, `issues/`, `map.md`, and `DEVELOPER.md` files preserve design
contracts, ticket evidence, and maintainer notes. They are **internal
non-user documentation**. They may describe earlier prototypes, conditional
claims, source-only research, unresolved evidence, or future work. They are not
v1 compatibility guarantees and should not be used instead of the public
README or `docs/` guides.

Public release documentation is limited to:

- [README](../README.md) — landing page, installation, configuration, tools,
  and quickstart.
- [Security](security.md) — cryptographic and secret-boundary details.
- [CI, release, and compatibility](ci-release.md) — supported versions,
  workflow behavior, and migration notes.
- [v1.0.0 release notes](release-notes-v1.0.0.md) — shipped scope and known
  operational boundaries.
